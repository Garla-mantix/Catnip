Our story

The Cat Shop was founded in 2024 by a passionate cat lover with a dream of connecting wonderful cat breeds with loving homes across Sweden.

We work with responsible breeders from around the world to offer healthy, well-socialized cats of the highest quality. Every cat sold through us comes with complete health information and breed history.

Our mission is simple: to help you find your perfect cat and give it a loving home.
